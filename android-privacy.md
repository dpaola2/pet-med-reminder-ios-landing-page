# Pet Med Reminder for Android: Privacy Policy
Updated November 23, 2026

Pet Med Reminder Tracker is published by Enginery. Contact: dpaola2@gmail.com.

## Records on your phone
Pet names, species, care item names, dose labels, schedules, dose history and photos are stored in the app's private storage. You do not create an account. There is no live sync with another Android phone or with the iPhone app. Some of this information is also sent as usage data, as described below.

## Backup and photos
If you enable Android backup, the Android operating system can back up the app database and its randomly generated caregiver identifier through your Google account. Android controls backup availability, timing, retention and restoration. Encrypted cloud backup is required on Android 12 and later. Database photo thumbnails are included; full-resolution photos are excluded from both cloud backup and device transfer. Restored pet profiles can display their thumbnails. We cannot access your Google account backup.

The system Photo Picker grants access only to a photo you select. The app does not request access to your photo library as a whole, camera, contacts or location.

## Usage data
Release builds on real devices send usage data to PostHog in the United States over HTTPS. We use it to improve the app. It includes:

- the names and species of your pets, and whether a pet has a photo
- the names of your medicines and care items, dose labels, the vet's instructions you enter, and the food rule (with food, before food or after food)
- schedules: how often, the times of day, how long a course lasts, and the steps of a taper
- the scheduled time and the actual time of each dose, with your time zone offset, and what you logged (given, skipped or changed later)
- text you type in the app
- screen recordings of the app with nothing hidden, including all text on screen, what you type and pet photos
- the screens you open and the buttons you use
- when you open, close, install or update the app, the reminders it has scheduled, and the hours since you last opened it
- what you do with a reminder notification (open it, mark it given, skip it, snooze it or dismiss it)
- your answers to the app's notification question and to the notification permission request, and whether notifications are allowed
- what you change when you edit or delete a pet or care item
- when you stop a care item or every reminder for a pet, and the reason you choose, including that a pet died
- when a course of medicine finishes, and how many of its doses were given, skipped or missed
- when you share a care sheet: the pet, the names of the care items on it and the app you chose in the share sheet
- when you tap Share Pet Med Reminder, and the app you chose in the share sheet
- when you open History or export a PDF, the number of days and doses in it
- when you tap Rate Pet Med Reminder, and when the app asks Google Play to show its rating card
- the number of pets, care items and doses, the species of your pets, the app version and the first app version you used, kept with this installation's profile
- whether you marked this phone as a test device in Settings
- device and app details, such as the app version, Android version, device maker and model, screen size, language, time zone, network type and mobile carrier, and whether the app was installed from Google Play
- app logs, errors and crash reports
- an approximate location that PostHog derives from your IP address

PostHog ties this data to a random identifier for this installation and to session identifiers. We do not ask for your name or email, and no advertising identifier is requested. We do not use this data for advertising and we do not sell it. PostHog processes it for product analytics on our behalf. Debug builds and detected emulators send nothing.

## Ratings
After you have logged doses on several days, the app may ask Google Play once to show its rating card. Google Play decides whether the card appears and handles any rating you leave under its own policies. The app is not told whether you rated it. Settings also has a Rate Pet Med Reminder row that opens the app's Google Play page.

## PDF sharing
When you choose Export PDF, a report is generated locally. It contains the pet names, care item names, scheduled times, statuses and actual times in your chosen range, and the day each care item or pet in it stopped, with the reason for a care item. It leaves the app only when you choose a destination in Android's share sheet. That destination handles the copy under its own policies.

## Care sheet sharing
When you choose Care sheet for a sitter on a pet, a one-page PDF is generated locally. It contains the pet's name and, for each active care item, its name, amount, schedule, food rule, start and end dates, taper steps and the vet's instructions you entered, and a QR code that opens petmedreminder.com. It leaves the app only when you choose a destination in Android's share sheet. That destination handles the copy under its own policies.

## Retention and deletion
Local records remain until you delete a pet or care item, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Deleting a pet also deletes that pet's care items and dose history. Android backups and the PDF copies and care sheets you share are separate copies; use Google's backup controls or the destination app to manage them. Uninstalling does not delete data already sent to PostHog. Usage data may be kept for product analysis until we delete it from our analytics project. Contact us with privacy or deletion questions. We do not collect your name or email in the app, so we cannot find your installation from an email address alone.

## Platform diagnostics
Google Play and Android may provide crash and performance diagnostics under your device and Google account settings. These can contain technical information about a crash or device.

## Children
The app is intended for adult pet caregivers. It is not directed to children under 13.

## Policy changes
We will update this policy and its date when Android data practices change. Contact dpaola2@gmail.com with questions.
