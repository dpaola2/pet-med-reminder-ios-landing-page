Pet Med Reminder for Android is in testing.

Pet Med Reminder reminds you when each dose is due and keeps a log of every dose to show your vet. It is free on the iPhone. Google needs a group of testers before a new app can go on Google Play, and you can be one of them.

  1. Join the testers group with the Google account you use on your Android phone.
  2. When the test opens, I post a link in the group. Open it on your phone, tap “Become a tester”, and install the app from Google Play.
  3. Keep the app on your phone for at least 14 days. That is Google’s rule for new apps.
Join the testers group

Only I post in the group, and other members cannot see your email address. You can leave it at any time.

No account, no signup in the app. Your records stay on your phone. The app also sends usage data, and the privacy policy lists all of it. Android privacy policy
