Did everyone
get everything?

One tap for the whole round.

Pet Med Reminder puts every dose that is due at the same time, for all your pets, into one round. You get one reminder for the round, and you mark it given with one tap.

iPhone and iPad. No account.

One reminder per round.

At 6:00 PM, four pets and seven items send one notification. It names each pet and what each one gets. Mark all given from the notification. If one pet did not take a dose, open the app and mark that dose by itself.

Missed a few days?

Today shows the doses that you did not log in the last 14 days. One sheet settles them: all at once, a day at a time, or one dose at a time. Reminders are set up to 14 days ahead, so they keep coming after a busy week.

Day 9 of 14, for every kitten.

Give a course an end date, and each row counts the days. When the course ends, its reminders stop.

Every pet, every med, one list.

Add as many pets as you keep, and as many items as each one takes: pills, drops, chews, supplements, and monthly flea and heartworm doses. If you give a monthly dose late, the next one counts from the day you gave it.

Show what was given.

Export any date range as a PDF. It lists each dose with the time it was due and the time it was given. Bring it to the vet, or send it to the rescue.

Questions

Does it tell me what dose to give?

No. It records what your vet told you to give, and when you gave it.

Can two people use it?

It syncs across your own devices through your iCloud account. Two people with different Apple IDs cannot share one household.

Is there an Android version?

No. The app is for iPhone and iPad.

What does the app send you?

Anonymous usage data and, from version 1.12.0, session recordings with photos blocked out. The privacy policy lists everything.

iPhone and iPad. No account.
