Your veterinarian suggested keeping a record of your pet’s medication.

Pet Med Reminder is a free iPhone app that reminds you when a dose is due and keeps a log you can bring to the next appointment.

Taking you to the App Store…

View on the App Store

Not on an iPhone? The app is iPhone-only for now. A paper log works too: write down each dose as you give it, and bring it with you to the recheck. What matters is that your vet can see what actually happened between visits.

No account, no signup. Your pet’s information stays on your own device and your own iCloud. Privacy policy
