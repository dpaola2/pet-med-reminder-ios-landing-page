Your veterinarian suggested keeping a record of your pet’s medication.

Pet Med Reminder is a free app for iPhone and Android. It reminds you when a dose is due and keeps a log you can bring to the next appointment.

Taking you to the App Store…

iPhone: App Store Android: join the testers

The Android version is in testing. Google needs a group of testers before a new app can go on Google Play, and the testers page shows how to get it. A paper log works too: write down each dose as you give it, and bring it with you to the recheck. What matters is that your vet can see what actually happened between visits.

No account, no signup. Your records stay on your phone, and on iPhone also in your own iCloud. The app also sends usage data, including what you enter, and the privacy policy lists all of it. iPhone privacy policy · Android privacy policy
